How to practice

  • Latin to English. Read the Latin and type one meaning from memory.
  • English to Latin. Read the meaning and type one Latin form. The short form is enough for prepositions. Turn on Require macrons if you want to practice them.
  • Learn. Review all 30 entries. Hide the meanings and tap a card to check yourself.
  • Word Bank. Pick the answer from a list.
  • Match. Find five Latin and English pairs.
  • Stars. Earn a star by answering correctly on your first try. Three stars means you have mastered that prompt.

Try 10 questions in each direction. After each round, choose Practice these to review the ones you missed.

Spelling counts. Capital letters and spaces are optional. You can type English verbs with or without “to.”
