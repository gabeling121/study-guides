🏛️ Mesopotamia & Sumer

This study guide covers your textbook section on the first civilizations in Mesopotamia and the Sumerians. Use the buttons at the top.

Learn
Every section of the reading, boiled down to the key facts, plus the Key Words with how to say them. Use the buttons at the top to jump to a section. You can also print a key words worksheet and its answer key.
Quiz
Tap the right answer from the word bank. Pick a quick round of 10, just the key words, or all the questions. If you miss one, the app shows you the facts so you can learn it.
Spelling ★ (the hard one!)
Read the clue and type the word, like cuneiform, ziggurat, or Euphrates. Spelling counts, but capital letters don't. A misspelled word shows the right spelling with the wrong letters in red. Type it correctly to keep going.
Match (game)
Tap a key word, then tap its meaning. Match all 6 pairs as fast as you can and try to beat your best time.
Map (bonus)
See where it all happened: the Tigris and Euphrates, the Persian Gulf, the Fertile Crescent, and the cities of Ur, Uruk, and Eridu. Tap Find it! to quiz yourself. This probably won't be on the test, but it helps the rest make sense.
Stars
Get a question right on the first try and you earn a star. Miss it and you lose one. 3 stars means you've mastered it. The app always asks the ones you know least first.

A good way to study

  1. Read Learn from top to bottom (it's short!).
  2. Play Match a few times to learn the key words.
  3. Do Quiz rounds until most questions have stars.
  4. Finish with Spelling ★.
  5. The night before: print the key words worksheet and fill it in from memory.

A little practice each day works better than one long session the night before. Good luck!
