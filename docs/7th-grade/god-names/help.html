Memorize the name pairs

A short study plan

1. Learn: read the 12 pairs, hide the answers, and tap each card to check yourself. Choose Greek to Roman to reverse the cards.

2. Match: pair clues and answers.

3. Word Bank or Multiple Choice: practice Roman to Greek, Greek to Roman, or both.

4. Written Answers: recall the name without a word bank. Spelling counts; spaces, punctuation, and capitalization do not. Jupiter or Jove both count. Apollo or Phoebus both count. You can write alternate names in either order, such as Jove/Jupiter. Wrong extra names do not count.

Complete a round in each direction, then practice missed questions and weak spots. Three stars means mastered in that mode. Matching is practice and does not change stars.

Print the worksheet and answer key from Learn or the 7th-grade page. This guide uses only the 12 pairs you supplied.
