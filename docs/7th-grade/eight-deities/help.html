Learn these eight deities

Review, then practice

Review Cards: look at one deity’s name, recall the Greek name, realm, and symbols, then tap to reveal the facts. Use Previous, Next card, or Shuffle.

Practice this card focuses on the deity you just reviewed. Practice lets you identify deities from names, realms, and symbols by tapping one of four answers. Choose a category or mixed practice.

Under More, you can use matching, a larger word bank, written answers, and stars. Spelling counts for written answers; spaces, capitalization, and incidental punctuation do not. Alternate Roman names in the chart qualify.

“Name every” questions in the extra modes require all listed items, in any order. Separate items with commas or semicolons. Wrong extra items make the answer incorrect.

Caelus and Vesta Prisca/Tellus have no symbols listed on this chart. No symbols were added for them. Saturn’s realm is harvest and later Time. Luna’s cow-drawn chariot includes crescent horns.

After a miss, read the correction and practice the missed question again. Three stars means mastered in that quiz mode. Your progress belongs to this guide and is separate from the twelve-deity guide.
