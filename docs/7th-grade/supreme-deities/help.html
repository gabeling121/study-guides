Master the deity chart

Study one part at a time

Start with Roman and Greek names. Then learn realms, symbols, and the Roman Trinity and family relationships.

Review Cards: study one deity at a time. Recall the Greek name, realms, and symbols, then tap the card to reveal the facts. Use Next card, Previous, or Shuffle. Practice this card asks identification questions about that deity.

Match: pair clues with their answers. Under More, Word Bank lets you choose from a larger bank. Practice: identify the deity by tapping one of four answers. Choose names, realms, symbols, or mixed practice. Written Answers: write from memory without a word bank.

Spelling counts; spaces, capitalization, and incidental punctuation do not. The chart’s alternate names count, such as Jove for Jupiter and Phoebus for Apollo.

A “name every” question needs all listed items. Their order does not matter. Separate them with commas or semicolons. “Bow and arrow” counts for “bow/arrow,” and “helmet and shield” counts for “helmet/shield.” A wrong extra item makes the answer incorrect.

Some realms and symbols belong to more than one deity. When the question asks for one deity, any correct Roman deity listed for that clue counts.

After a written miss, read the correction, type a correct answer, and tap Next. Practice missed questions and weak spots. Three stars means mastered in that quiz mode. Matching does not change stars.

The short practice sheet and key provide a review sample. Full question and answer booklets cover every question. This guide uses only your supplied chart.
