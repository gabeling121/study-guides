Twelve Supreme Deities were in the Roman Pantheon. The Roman Trinity consisted of Jupiter, Juno, and Minerva.

| Roman | Greek | Realm | Symbols |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jupiter/Jove | Zeus | King of the gods | Eagle, oak leaves, thunderbolts |
| Neptune | Poseidon | Sea, earthquakes | Trident, dolphins, horses |
| Vulcan | Hephaestus | Gods’ armor maker, fire | Anvil, forge |
| Juno | Hera | Queen of the gods, married women | Peacock, pomegranate, cuckoo, cow, lion |
| Apollo (Phoebus) | Apollo | Sun, music, poetry, athletes, prophecy | Sun chariot, bow/arrow, laurel leaves, lyre, mouse (twin of Diana/Artemis) |
| Mercury | Hermes | Messenger, theft, commerce, guide of the dead | Caduceus, winged cap/sandals |
| Mars | Ares | War | Shield, spear, dog, vultures |
| Ceres | Demeter | Agriculture, grain | Wheat, poppies, cornucopia (daughter = Persephone/Proserpina) |
| Diana | Artemis | Hunting, moon, virgins | Bow/arrow, stag, crescent, she-bear |
| Minerva | Athena | Wisdom, war, weaving | Aegis, owl, helmet/shield, olive tree |
| Venus | Aphrodite | Love and beauty | Doves, sparrows, female charms |
| Vesta | Hestia | Hearth, home | Hearth fire |
