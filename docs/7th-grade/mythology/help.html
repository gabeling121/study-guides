How to study mythology

A little at a time

Choose a section. Start with the twelve supreme deities, then try heroes and creatures.

Learn: read the cards. Hide facts, say what you remember, then tap to check. The complete supplied notes are linked below the cards.

Match: pair clues with answers. These rounds do not change stars.

Word Bank: tap from a larger bank. Multiple Choice: choose from four answers.

Written Answers: write from memory with no word bank. Capitalization, punctuation, and missing or extra spaces do not count. Spelling does. The sheet’s alternate names count, such as Jove for Jupiter. Short equivalent wording for a fact also counts when listed after a miss.

“Name all” questions need every item. Order does not matter unless the question says “in order.” Use commas or semicolons between items. The twelve labors must be in order when asked that way. A wrong extra item makes an answer incorrect.

After a miss, read the answer and type it correctly to continue. Then tap Next. Three stars means mastered in that mode. Word Bank, Multiple Choice, and Written Answers each keep their own stars. Practice weak spots or the questions you missed.

The practice sheet and key each give a short review sample. Print all questions for the full booklet. Every detail is available in the cards, supplied notes, and question bank.

This guide follows your teacher’s supplied sheet, including its names, spellings, family relationships, and story versions. No outside mythology facts were added. Accepted equivalent answers only rephrase the same facts; they do not change the sheet.
