Your grammar practice plan

Learn: Read the present-active chart, conjugation identifiers, and singular noun endings. Hide the chart answers and say them from memory. Tap a review card for explanations.

Multiple choice: Choose one answer. Fill in the blank ★: Type your answer from memory with no word bank. Use Focus to practice grammar, the verb chart, conjugations, noun charts, or third-declension endings.

Questions and choices shuffle each round. Practice weak spots and Practice these also shuffle. A random order may occasionally repeat.

Macrons count: Use ā ē ī ō ū buttons to insert a letter at the cursor or replace selected text. Missing or extra macrons are incorrect. Capitals, spacing, punctuation, and the hyphen before an ending do not count.

For English pronouns, any listed alternative counts: he, she, or it. You may supply several valid alternatives in any order. For 2nd-person plural, you, you all, y’all, or you (plural) count. For a question asking all SIX endings, include every ending once in any order, separated by commas, slashes, semicolons, and, or, or &.

After a miss, the correct spelling appears with differences in red. Retype an accepted answer to continue. First-try correct earns one star; a miss loses one star. Three stars means mastered. Progress is saved only in this browser on this device.

What was added: Standard definitions and answers complete the blank PDF charts. The 1st-person singular regular ending is -ō; -m occurs in some verbs, such as sum. The 2nd-declension nominative singular chart uses -us / -er. Your confirmed third-declension list is -er, -or, -is, -x, -ō, -ns. This is the class list, not an exhaustive rule for identifying declensions.

The conjugation practice tests identifying endings, not full verb paradigms. Examples follow the photo, including labōrō and dormīs. dormīs is 2nd-person singular. No example was provided for 3rd -iō.

Grammar typed questions ask for the term instead of requiring a long definition word for word. Multiple choice also checks the meanings. First/second/third/fourth and numbered forms are accepted where appropriate. No fuzzy spelling matches are used.
