How to play

Which story is it?

Choose Heroes & stories, Creatures & monsters, Hercules’ Labors, or a mixed round. Read the description, then tap the correct title. The answer’s own name is hidden in the clue; names of other characters remain.

Use a short 10-question round or play every description. After a miss, read the named description. Finish the round, then practice your missed questions.

Review Cards shows one title at a time. Recall its story, tap to reveal the complete description, then use Next, Previous, or Shuffle.

Under More, try matching, a larger title bank, written titles, or stars. Spelling counts for written titles; spaces, case, and incidental punctuation do not. For paired titles, write both names; their order does not matter.

First-try correct earns a star; a miss loses one. Three stars means mastered in that mode. Your progress is saved separately from the other study guides.

The supplied names, details, and story versions are preserved. The game replaces the answer’s name with a role such as “the hero” or “the king” so the clue does not give it away. Creature-family entries are included as their own titles. Pegasus’s paragraph remains with Chimaera, as in your material. No extra mythology facts were added.

Print a short clue sheet or the full descriptions and answer booklets.

Hercules’ labors in order

Choose Hercules’ Labors to practice all twelve. Questions go both ways: name the labor from its number, or choose the number from the labor’s name. Review Cards hides each labor behind its number.

For the hardest challenge, open Written Answers under More. The all-twelve question requires the complete sequence in order. Separate names with commas or semicolons.
