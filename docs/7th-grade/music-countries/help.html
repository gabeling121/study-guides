How to study

Start with the eight pairs

Learn: tap a style to show or hide its country. Use Hide countries to test yourself. Match: choose each country's name. Dominican Republic is used twice.

Quiz

Practice both directions: style to country and country to style. Dominican Republic needs BOTH Bachata and Merengue. The word bank includes them together.

Spelling ★

Type the style or country. Spelling counts; capitals, spaces and incidental punctuation do not. For Dominican Republic, type both Bachata and Merengue in either order, separated by and, or, a comma, slash, semicolon, or &. A single style is incomplete for this question; extra incorrect styles are not accepted. No alternate country pairings or spelling synonyms have been added.

After a miss, red letters show the spelling to fix. Retype the answer correctly before going on.

Stars

First-try correct: +1 star. A miss: -1 star. Three stars means mastered. Quiz and spelling have separate stars. Weakest questions come first. Practice these reviews misses after a round. Practice weak spots focuses on unmastered questions.

A short study plan

Read the cards, complete matching, try a quiz, then practice spelling. Stars stay in this browser. Visit online once to save the guide for offline use. Add the guide to your home screen if you like.

Printables and content

Learn includes a blank practice sheet and answer key. The guide uses exactly the eight assigned pairings; no extra test facts were added. These pairings are study targets, not a full account of each style's history.
